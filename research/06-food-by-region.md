# 06 · Food by Region

Taiwan is a food trip disguised as a road trip. Everywhere: 7-Eleven / FamilyMart
(tea eggs, onigiri, hot lunch boxes, coffee), breakfast shops (蛋餅 danbing,
豆漿 soy milk), bubble tea, and night markets.

| Stop | Must-try | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taipei | Beef noodle soup (牛肉麵), xiaolongbao, pepper buns (胡椒餅), braised pork rice (滷肉飯) | Raohe / Ningxia night markets |
| Keelung | Seafood, pot-side scrapings (鼎邊趖), tempura (天婦羅) | Miaokou night market |
| Jiufen | Taro balls (芋圓), fish balls | Jiufen Old Street |
| Yilan / Luodong | Scallion pancakes (蔥油餅), smoked duck (鴨賞), cherry duck, niu-she-bing (牛舌餅) | Luodong night market |
| Su'ao | Mackerel, seafood at Nanfang'ao harbour | Nanfang'ao |
| Hualien | Bianshi wontons (扁食), mochi (麻糬), Indigenous bamboo-tube rice (竹筒飯), stinky tofu | Dongdamen night market |
| Rift Valley | Chishang lunch box (池上便當), Ruisui milk, Yuli stinky tofu, Guanshan bento | Chishang, Guanshan |
| East coast Hwy 11 | Chenggong fresh sashimi / swordfish, Dulan cafés, Amis cuisine | Chenggong fishing harbour, Dulan |
| Taitung | Sugar apple (釋迦, custard apple), fried rice noodles, Indigenous millet wine | Tiehua village, Taitung night markets (check operating days locally) |
| Kenting | Seafood, onion from Hengchun, mango ice (summer) | Kenting Main Street night market |
| Donggang | Bluefin tuna (in season May–Jun), sakura shrimp, "three treasures" | Huaqiao seafood market |
| Kaohsiung | Papaya milk, seafood, duck rice | Ruifeng / Liuhe night markets, Cijin island |
| Tainan | Beef soup (牛肉湯) breakfast, danzai noodles (擔仔麵), milkfish congee, shrimp rolls, coffin bread (棺材板), oyster omelette | Garden night market (Thu/Sat/Sun), Guohua St |
| Chiayi | Turkey rice (雞肉飯), fish-head casserole (砂鍋魚頭) | Wenhua Road night market |
| Changhua / Lukang | Bawan (肉圓), Lukang ox-tongue cakes, oyster omelette | Lukang Old Street |
| Taichung | Bubble tea birthplace (Chun Shui Tang), sun cakes (太陽餅), Fengjia night market | Fengjia, Yizhong St |
| Sun Moon Lake | Thao-style dishes, Assam black tea, president fish (曲腰魚) | Ita Thao |
| Alishan | High-mountain oolong, wasabi, bento | Alishan village |
| Hsinchu | Rice noodles (米粉), meatballs (貢丸), Hakka lei cha (擂茶) in Beipu | City God Temple area |
| Sanyi | Hakka food, hakka mochi | Sanyi old street |

Diet notes for the app:
* **Vegetarian**: Taiwan has many Buddhist vegetarian buffets (素食, 自助餐 with a 卍 sign).
* **Halal**: limited outside Taipei/Taichung/Kaohsiung — app can surface Muslim-friendly restaurant search tip.
* Water: tap water is not usually drunk; refill machines in hostels and 7-Elevens sell water.
