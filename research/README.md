## Confidence & freshness

* Items marked **⚠ VERIFY** change often (road closures, trial openings, prices).
  The app surfaces the same warnings to users and links to official sources.
* Distances are approximate road distances for scooter-legal roads (no freeways),
  rounded to the nearest 5 km. They are good for planning, not navigation.
* Prices are in New Taiwan Dollars (TWD / NT$). ~NT$30 ≈ US$1 (2026).
