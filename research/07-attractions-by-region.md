# 07 · Attractions by Region

Seeded in `src/data/attractions.ts` (id, stop, category, time, cost, lat/lng).
Coordinates are approximate (±200 m) — good for maps, not for pin-point nav.

## North
* **Taipei 101 / Elephant Mountain** — skyline hike, sunset. 2 h. Free (101 observatory NT$600).
* **Yehliu Geopark** — queen's head rock formations on Tai 2. 1.5 h. NT$120.
* **Jiufen Old Street** — hillside gold-mining town, teahouses. 2–3 h. Free. Crowded weekends.
* **Bitou Cape trail** — cliffside loop on NE coast. 1.5 h. Free.
* **Fulong Beach** — golden sand; sand sculpture festival (May–Jul). 1 h. Beach fee in season.
* **Guishan Island view / Toucheng** — Wai'ao beach surfing. 1 h.
* **Jiaoxi hot springs** (Yilan) — free foot baths, public baths. 1–2 h.
* **National Center for Traditional Arts** (Wujie, Yilan). 2 h. NT$150.
* **Nanfang'ao harbour** — fishing port & lookout. 1 h.
* **Tamsui** — sunset, Fort San Domingo, old street. 2 h.
* **Pingxi / Shifen** — sky lanterns, waterfall. 2–3 h. (Detour from Tai 2/Tai 106.)

## East – Hualien
* **Qingshui Cliffs** — view from Huide viewpoint on Tai 9. 30 min. Free.
* **Taroko Gorge** — partially open, check status. Half day.
* **Qixingtan Beach** — pebble crescent beach north of the city. 1 h.
* **Dongdamen night market**. Evening.
* **Baqi viewpoint (芭崎)** — Hwy 11 sea view. 20 min.
* **Shitiping** — stepped volcanic rocks, campsite. 1 h.
* **Tropic of Cancer marker (Jingpu)** — 15 min.
* **Rift Valley:** Liyu Lake, Mataian wetland (Guangfu), Ruisui hot springs, Yuli, **Chishang Mr Brown Avenue / Brown Boulevard** (rent a bicycle through rice fields), **Luye Highland** (hot-air balloons Jul–Aug).

## East – Taitung
* **Baxian Caves** — prehistoric cave site. 1 h.
* **Sanxiantai** — 8-arch dragon footbridge to islet. 1.5 h. Sunrise spot.
* **Dulan** — surf/art village, sugar factory art space. Evening.
* **Jialulan** — seaside park, driftwood art. 30 min.
* **Taitung Forest Park / Seaside Park** — 1–2 h.
* **Zhiben hot springs** — 2 h.
* **Taimali** — golden needle mountain (day lily flowers Aug–Sep), first sunrise spot.
* **Green Island / Orchid Island** — ferry from Fugang harbour. 1–3 days.

## South
* **Shouka / South Link viewpoints** — Pacific to Taiwan Strait in one ride.
* **Jiupeng desert dunes / Gangzai dunes** (east Pingtung) — ATV tours.
* **Kenting:** Eluanbi Lighthouse (NT$60), Longpan Park (cliff grassland, sunrise), Sail Rock, Baisha beach (Life of Pi), Kenting Main Street night market, Kenting Forest Recreation Area (NT$150), Guanshan sunset (Hengchun).
* **Sichongxi hot springs** (Checheng).
* **Donggang / Xiaoliuqiu** — sea turtles, Vase Rock. 1 day.
* **Kaohsiung:** Lotus Pond (Dragon & Tiger Pagodas), Pier-2 Art Center, Cijin Island (ferry, scooter allowed), Fo Guang Shan (Buddha museum, free).
* **Tainan:** Anping Old Fort & Tree House, Chihkan Tower, Confucius Temple, Shennong Street, Chimei Museum, Qigu salt mountain, Sicao green tunnel (boat).
* **Beimen / Budai** — crystal wedding church, salt fields.

## Central & West
* **Chiayi:** Hinoki Village, Chiayi Park, art museum.
* **Alishan:** Sunrise at Zhushan, giant tree trail, forest railway. Full day.
* **Sun Moon Lake:** lakeside cycling path (one of the world's best), boat, ropeway to Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village, Xiangshan visitor centre. Full day.
* **Lukang:** Longshan Temple, Nine-Turns Lane, Mazu temple. 2 h.
* **Changhua:** Great Buddha, Fan-shaped roundhouse. 1–2 h.
* **Gaomei Wetlands** (Taichung coast) — sunset, windmills. 1.5 h.
* **Taichung:** Rainbow Village, National Opera House, Fengjia night market, Calligraphy Greenway.
* **Sanyi:** woodcarving museum, Longteng broken bridge. 2 h.
* **Hsinchu:** Old city god temple, Neiwan old street (inland), Beipu Hakka village.
