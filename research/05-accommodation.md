# 05 · Accommodation

## Types
* **Hostels (青年旅館)** — Taipei, Taichung, Tainan, Kaohsiung, Hualien, Taitung, Kenting all have good ones. Dorms NT$350–900. Great for solo riders to meet other huandao-ers.
* **Minsu (民宿, B&B)** — The classic east-coast stay. Family-run, often with breakfast, sea or rice-field views. NT$1,200–3,000 per room. Many only list on Booking/Agoda or Facebook/LINE. Scooter parking is usually easy.
* **Budget business hotels** — near train stations in every city. NT$1,200–2,200.
* **Motels (汽車旅館)** — ride straight into a private garage. Surprisingly good value for 2 people on a bike (NT$1,500–3,000), common on city outskirts.
* **Resorts / hot-spring hotels** — Ruisui, Zhiben (Taitung), Jiaoxi (Yilan), Sichongxi (Pingtung). NT$3,000–8,000.
* **Camping** — Taiwan has many paid campsites (露營區), often on farms. Wild camping on beaches is officially discouraged; Kenting/national parks prohibit it.
* **Temples** — historically cyclists could sometimes sleep at temples if they asked politely; don't rely on this.

## Booking tips
* Book ahead for **Fri/Sat nights, Kenting in summer, Chinese New Year, long weekends (e.g., 228, Tomb Sweeping, Dragon Boat, Moon Festival, National Day)** — prices can double.
* Weekdays on the east coast: walk-ins usually fine, but small towns (Dulan, Chishang) have limited rooms.
* Always ask: *scooter parking?* (機車停車), *check-in time?* (many minsu want arrival by 20:00).
* Free cancellation bookings are useful — plans change with weather.
