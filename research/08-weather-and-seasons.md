## Key facts
* **Typhoon season:** May–Oct, peak Jul–Sep. East coast is hit first. A typhoon can close the Suhua, Hwy 11, South Link for 1–3 days. Monitor the **Central Weather Administration** (CWA). Rentals may lock the scooter during a typhoon warning.
* **Plum rain (梅雨):** mid-May to mid-June, persistent heavy showers.
* **NE monsoon:** Oct–Mar, cold drizzle in the north and on the Yilan coast; strong headwinds on the west coast for clockwise riders; strong downslope winds (落山風) in Kenting/Hengchun in winter.
* **Heat:** Jul–Aug 33–36 °C; ride early (06:00–11:00), rest midday.
* **Daylight:** Sunset ~17:10 (Dec) to ~18:50 (Jun). Avoid riding the Suhua/mountains after dark.
* **Rain gear:** buy a two-piece raincoat & shoe covers (NT$150–400) at 7-Eleven.

## Public holidays that spike prices / traffic
Chinese New Year (Jan/Feb), 228 Peace Day (Feb 28), Tomb Sweeping (early Apr), Labour Day (May 1), Dragon Boat (May/Jun),
Moon Festival (Sep/Oct), Teachers' Day (Sep 28), National Day (Oct 10), Retrocession Day (Oct 25), Constitution Day (Dec 25).
Teachers', Retrocession and Constitution Day became days off again from 2025/26; Labour Day is a day off for everyone from 2026.
Rule: a holiday on Saturday → day off the Friday before; on Sunday → the Monday after. No make-up workdays in 2026 or 2027.
