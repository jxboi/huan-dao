## Accident procedure (tell users in-app)
1. Make safe: hazard lights, move off road if possible, don't move injured people.
2. Call **110** (police must record every accident for insurance) and **119** if injured.
3. Photograph positions, plates, damage *before* moving vehicles.
4. Call the rental shop.
5. Get the police accident report number (道路交通事故當事人登記聯單).

## Common dangers
* **Trucks & gravel trucks** on Suhua / Tai 9 — give space, don't overtake in tunnels.
* **Dogs** — stray dogs on rural roads, esp. east coast at night.
* **Sand, gravel & wet paint** on corners and road markings; manhole covers slippery in rain.
* **Blind mountain corners** — sound horn on single-lane mountain roads.
* **Sudden U-turns / door-opening** in towns.
* **Heat stroke / dehydration** — 7-Eleven every few km; drink.
* **Fatigue** — keep daily ride ≤6 h for most people; stop every 60–90 min.
* **Night riding** — rural roads are unlit; plan to arrive before dark.
* **Earthquakes** — if riding: slow down, stop away from cliffs/tunnels.

## Packing checklist (seeded in app)
Documents: passport, IDP + home licence, rental contract, insurance card, cash (NT$), credit card, EasyCard (iPASS).
Riding: two-piece raincoat, shoe covers, gloves, buff/face mask, sunglasses, sunscreen, phone mount, power bank + cables, bungee cords / cargo net, small lock.
Clothing: quick-dry shirts, light fleece (mountains/winter), sandals, swimwear.
Health: basic first aid, painkillers, motion-sickness pills (mountain passengers), mosquito repellent, prescription meds.
Tech: eSIM/SIM with data, offline maps (Google Maps offline area, Maps.me/Organic Maps), translation app.
Extras: small towel, laundry bag, earplugs (hostels), dry bags, 環島 flag/sticker!
