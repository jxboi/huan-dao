# 01 · Overview & Routes

## What is Huan Dao?

環島 (huándǎo) = "around the island". A rite of passage for young Taiwanese,
popularised by the 2006 film *Island Etude* (練習曲). Done by bicycle, scooter,
car, train or on foot. Riders often put a "環島中" / "環島ing" sticker or flag on
their bike — expect friendly honks and waves on the east coast.

The official cycling loop (Taiwan Cycling Route No.1) is **~968 km**. A scooter
loop following the main scooter-legal highways is **~1,000–1,200 km** depending
on variants and detours. Most scooter riders take **7–14 days**; 5 days is a
hard push, 20+ days is a slow, detour-rich trip.

## Direction

| | Counter-clockwise (west first, south, then up the east) | Clockwise (east first) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sea views on east coast | **You ride in the sea-side lane** (traffic drives on the right) | Sea is across the road |
| Save the best for last | East coast at the end | East coast at the start, flat west at the end |
| Winter NE monsoon | Tailwind on the west coast | Headwind on the west coast |
| Suhua Hwy | Ridden at the end (you can decide based on weather) | Ridden on day 1–2 |

Most guides recommend **counter-clockwise**. The app defaults to it but lets
users flip.

## Canonical loop (hubs)

Taipei → (west) Hsinchu → Taichung → Chiayi → Tainan → Kaohsiung → Kenting →
(South Link) Taitung → Hualien → (Suhua) Yilan → Taipei

## Section variants (what users can toggle in the app)

Distances are approximate, scooter-legal roads, rounded.

### Taipei ↔ Yilan
1. **Northeast Coast (Tai 2)** — Keelung, Jiufen, Bitou Cape, Fulong beach, Toucheng. ~120 km. Scenic, easy. *Default.*
2. **Beiyi Highway (Tai 9)** — via Pinglin tea country. ~70 km. Twisty mountain road, notorious for speeding bikes; fast and fun but ride carefully. Shortest.
3. **Full North Coast (Tai 2 from Tamsui)** — Tamsui, Jinshan, Yehliu, Keelung, then NE coast. ~180 km. For those with time.

### Yilan ↔ Hualien (Suhua)
Only one realistic road corridor. ~125 km. See `02-road-status-and-restrictions.md`
— scooters ≤250cc must use Tai 9D (old Suhua) plus designated tunnels; since 2026
the Daqingshui–Heren section of Tai 9D is abandoned and traffic is routed on Tai 9.
**Most dangerous section of the loop** (trucks, tunnels, rockfall). Ride in daylight
and good weather, check status the morning of.

### Hualien ↔ Taitung (the headline choice)
1. **Coastal Hwy 11** — Baqi viewpoint, Shitiping, Baxian Caves, Sanxiantai, Dulan. ~170 km. Pacific views all day. *Default.*
2. **East Rift Valley Hwy 9** — Guangfu, Ruisui hot springs, Yuli, Chishang (Mr Brown Avenue), Guanshan. ~165 km. Rice fields, mountains, cycling paths.
3. **Valley then coast (Hwy 64 Rui-Gang)** — Rift Valley to Ruisui, cross the Xiuguluan River gorge road to the coast, then Hwy 11 south. ~185 km. Best of both.

### Taitung ↔ Kenting
1. **South Link (Tai 9) + Tai 26 west side** — Taimali, Dawu, cross the Central Range at Shouka pass (460 m), descend to the Taiwan Strait, Checheng, Hengchun. ~140 km. *Default.*
2. **Wild east Pingtung coast** — to Shouka, then County Rd 199/199A via Mudan to Xuhai, then Tai 26 past the Jiupeng dunes and Gangzai to Manzhou and Kenting. ~150 km. Remote; few petrol stations — fuel up in Dawu. **⚠ VERIFY** exact road numbers before release (199A/Tai 26 connections).

### Kenting ↔ Kaohsiung
Tai 26 → Tai 1 via Fangliao & Donggang (ferry to Xiaoliuqiu). ~105 km.

### Kaohsiung ↔ Tainan
Tai 1 / Tai 17. ~50 km. Urban.

### Tainan ↔ Chiayi
1. **Tai 1 direct** ~65 km. *Default.*
2. **Salt coast (Tai 17/61 via Beimen)** — Qigu salt mountain, Beimen crystal church, Budai. ~95 km.

### Chiayi ↔ Taichung
1. **Plains via Lukang & Changhua** — Tai 1 / Tai 17. ~110 km. *Default.*
2. **Sun Moon Lake detour** — Tai 3 / Tai 21 inland to Sun Moon Lake then down to Taichung. ~170 km. Mountain roads.
3. **Alishan + Sun Moon Lake (advanced)** — Tai 18 up to Alishan (2,200 m), Tai 18/21 over Tataka (2,600 m) to Sun Moon Lake, then Taichung. ~245 km, cold, steep, fog. 125cc works but slow. Plan 2 days.

### Taichung ↔ Hsinchu
1. **Tai 1 / Tai 61 coast** ~100 km. *Default.* (Tai 61 expressway sections are banned for scooters — follow signs; Tai 1 is safe.)
2. **Hakka hills (Tai 3 / Tai 13 via Sanyi)** — woodcarving town Sanyi, Dahu strawberries, Beipu. ~115 km.

### Hsinchu ↔ Taipei
1. **Tai 1 via Taoyuan** ~80 km. Urban, traffic lights.
2. **West coast via Bali & Tamsui** — Tai 15/61 frontage roads, sunset at Tamsui. ~110 km.

## Popular side trips (extra days)
* **Taroko Gorge** (from Xincheng/Hualien) — partly reopened, controlled access. 0.5–1 day.
* **Green Island / Orchid Island** — ferry from Fugang (Taitung). Rent a scooter on the island. 1–3 days.
* **Xiaoliuqiu** — ferry from Donggang. Turtles, scooter the 12 km loop. 1 day.
* **Alishan / Sun Moon Lake** — see above.
* **Jiufen & Pingxi** (sky lanterns) — from Taipei, half day.
