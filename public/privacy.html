Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Huan Dao Planner is a free, open-source trip planner for riding around Taiwan. You can use all of it without an account. This page explains what happens to your data.

Without signing in

Your trip settings (dates, route choices, saved places, packing list) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. A share link contains the plan itself in the link; it is only sent to whoever you share it with.

If you sign in with Facebook

Signing in is optional and only used to sync your trip between your devices. We receive from Facebook:

  • your name and profile picture (shown on the sign-in card),
  • your email address, if you allow it, and your Facebook user ID (used by our sign-in provider to identify your account).

We store those account details plus your trip settings. We do not post to Facebook, read your friends or other Facebook data, show ads, sell data, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts sign-in and the database that stores synced trips.
  • Vercel hosts the website and may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser) for security and operations.
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map tiles; your browser requests them directly.

Links to Google Maps and Booking.com open those sites, which have their own privacy policies. The app uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Account details and your synced trip are kept until you delete your account. Signing out does not delete them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and synced trip yourself at any time — see how to delete your data. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/jxboi/huan-dao/issues.
