How to delete your data

Last updated 27 September 2026

Delete your account and synced trip

  1. Open Huan Dao Planner and sign in with Facebook.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner to open Your account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account (name, profile picture, email, Facebook ID) and the trip saved in it from our servers. The copy of your plan in the browser on that device stays there; to remove it too, tap Start over on the Trip tab or clear the site's data in your browser.

Remove the app's access in Facebook

In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find huandao and tap Remove. This stops the app from receiving anything further from Facebook. It does not delete what is already stored, so also use the steps above.

Can't sign in any more?

Open an issue at github.com/jxboi/huan-dao/issues asking for deletion (don't post personal details there), and we'll arrange it privately.
